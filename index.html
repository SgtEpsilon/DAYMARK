MODEL NOTES

The optimizer numerically samples a complete orbit and evaluates solar elevation for fixed surface coordinates. It supports ordinary rotation and synchronous rotation without requiring a separate planet type switch.

The result is intended as a practical in-game coordinate finder. Game-specific orbital conventions may differ from a physical astronomy model, so treat the output as an approximation unless the game's coordinate/epoch conventions are known.
